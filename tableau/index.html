About CV Tableau tool

Tableau maker

Here's a tool I vibecoded for easily creating and typesetting OT, HG and MaxEnt tableaux. Feel free to use it for your own purposes as well. It's not quite as powerful as Hayes' OTSoft, but this one works in your browser.

Winners, fatal violations, harmonies and probabilities are calculated automatically, but you can still change any of them by hand.

Tableau

Tips for filling in the tableau
  • Violations: type a number (2) or stars (**). Leave a cell empty for no violations. Enter and the arrow keys move between rows.
  • Constraint names: Max_{IO} gives a subscript and X^{2} a superscript. Small caps are added automatically.
  • Ranking (OT): the columns are ranked from left to right. The ┆ button marks two neighbouring constraints as unranked (dashed line).
  • Winners: ☞ goes on the optimal candidate. Tick Winner to say which candidate should win. If another candidate beats it, that candidate gets ☹. The intended winners also drive the comparative tableau, RCD and the Hasse diagram.
  • By hand: “Edit marks by hand” keeps the current ! marks and shading and lets you change them. Type *! into cells, and click cells in the preview to shade them.
  • Spreadsheets: you can paste a block of cells copied from Excel or Google Sheets into the grid.

Import

Choose a file you saved here (.json) or an OTSoft file (tab-delimited .txt). You can also paste either format, or cells copied from a spreadsheet in OTSoft layout: two header rows with the constraint names in column D onwards, then input, candidate, frequency and violations.
