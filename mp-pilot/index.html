MaxEnt study: review copy

Thank you for looking this over. This is a private copy of a short online experiment. I don't want to store your data on my own website, so you'll need to download the results to your computer once you've done the experiment. Once you have that, it would be great if you could send the results to mika.bradley.22@ucl.ac.uk.

Press here to run the experiment

Take part as a participant would. It takes 15–20 minutes and needs a keyboard, so use a laptop or desktop. At the end a CSV file downloads. Please note anything that looked wrong, confusing or too fast, and whether the instructions made the task clear.

Each run shows one of four versions, chosen at random, and each version contains about a quarter of the sentence pairs. To see a particular version, open 1, 2, 3 or 4.

Press here to review the stimuli

This page will show you every item in the study, including those that your run didn't show. You'll be able to mark it fine or flag it, with an optional comment if you like. Your marks are kept in the browser, so you can stop and come back. When you are done, please download your comments as a CSV and send them to me.

This page is not listed anywhere and search engines will not index it. It will be taken down after the review.
