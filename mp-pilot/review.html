Review the sentences

Each item shows what a participant reads first (the context), then the sentence with a gap and the two ways to fill it, as they appear in the experiment. Participants are told to imagine chatting with a friend: the context is something they have just said, and they choose how they would carry on.

For the study items, each item appears in two contexts: one where the stronger presupposition is satisfied, and one where it isn't. A participant sees one context.
