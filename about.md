# Mika Bradley

I'm an MPhil student in Linguistics at University College London, working on formal pragmatics, computational linguistics, and the philosophy of language.

My research concerns the interface between semantic and conceptual-intentional systems, adopting an internalist perspective where meanings are procedural instructions to access and assemble mental representations. I'm particularly interested in how speakers select and reason about alternatives in context.

Before my MPhil, I completed my BSc in Experimental Linguistics, also at UCL, with a thesis on 'quasi-count' nouns and mereotopological semantics.
